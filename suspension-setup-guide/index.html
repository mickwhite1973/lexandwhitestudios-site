SUSPENSION SETUP GUIDE

One bike.
One complete setup.

Guidance for motorcycles, Can-Am Spyders, trikes and specialty suspension.

Version 1.4 · No account required

Understand what your suspension is doing before you start turning clickers. Measure SAG, identify preload, compression and rebound, fine-tune the ride, and keep complete front and rear setups together under each bike.

MEASURE.ADJUST.RIDE.YOUR GARAGE. YOUR DEVICE.

PRACTICAL GUIDANCE

Get to know the adjustments.

Measure SAG Properly

Record wheel travel and L1, L2 and L3, compare riding-style targets, then see whether preload needs to go up, down or stay where it is.

Understand Compression

Learn what the top compression adjuster changes, what harsh potholes, dive and bottoming feel like, and which direction to test one step at a time.

Understand Rebound

Learn what the lower rebound adjuster changes and how to recognise kicking, bouncing and pack-down over repeated bumps.

One Bike, One Setup

Front and rear SAG, preload, compression and rebound remain together in one vehicle profile instead of creating separate copies of the same bike.

Search My Garage

Search your own bikes, current suspension settings, notes and test history. Public web settings are deliberately kept out of your setup.

Spyder & Trike Friendly

Includes wheel-travel guidance and an optional left/right front SAG comparison for vehicles with independent twin-front suspension.

A REPEATABLE PROCESS

Measure. Adjust. Test. Save.

01

Save the bike once

Add the motorcycle, Spyder, trike or specialty suspension and identify the fitted adjusters.

02

Set SAG first

Use wheel travel and consistent measuring points. Correct SAG with preload, not damping clickers.

03

Fine-tune one symptom

Choose the clearest ride problem, change one control by one small step, then repeat the same test.

04

Keep the result

Better settings update the bike. Every before-and-after test remains available in Test History.

PRIVACY FIRST

Your garage
belongs to you.

Suspension Setup Guide does not require an account for the current release. Vehicle profiles, measurements, clicker positions, notes and test history are stored locally on your device.

Export and import tools can be used for user-controlled backup. A future optional account system may add device syncing, but local use will remain the foundation of the guide.
