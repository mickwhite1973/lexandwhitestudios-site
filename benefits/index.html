How Member Benefits will work

Lex & White helps you discover an offer. The partner remains responsible for the product or service you buy.

1

See an approved offer

Offers are shown clearly in the Member Benefits area.

2

Choose to open it

You decide whether to visit the partner. We do not inject partner adverts into normal webpages.

3

Buy from the partner

The partner handles payment, delivery, warranty, returns, licensing and product support.

No browsing-history targeting

Member Benefits is being designed as a separate service. Your private browsing history is not needed to decide which partner offers to show.

  • Offers are selected by category, region and availability.
  • You choose whether to open a partner offer.
  • Partner analytics will remain separate from normal browsing history.
Affiliate disclosure: Lex & White Studios may receive a commission when a purchase is made through selected partner links. This does not increase the price you pay. Offers and availability may change.
