Partnership options

We can begin with whichever arrangement fits the partner’s existing commercial structure.

Option A

Member Discount Partner

Provide a selected discount, free-shipping offer, bundle or other useful customer benefit.

Option B

Affiliate Partner

Lex & White refers customers through an approved tracked link and receives commission on qualifying purchases.

Option C

Featured Partner

Receive additional placement within the Member Benefits area or agreed Lex & White communications.

Option D

Promotional Partner

Work together on a launch, member promotion, sponsored prize or other agreed campaign.

A simple commercial relationship

The partner stays in control of its product and customer transaction. Lex & White focuses on discovery and referral.

What partners can receive

  • Website exposure within Member Benefits.
  • Tracked customer referrals where supported.
  • Potential placement within selected Lex & White software.
  • Future joint campaign opportunities as our customer base grows.

What we do not need from partners

  • We do not need to hold your inventory.
  • We do not need to process your customer payments.
  • We do not take over your warranty or product support.
  • We do not need private browsing history.

We’re selecting foundation partners

We are at the early stage of building our customer base and will be straightforward about that. We would rather establish a small number of credible long-term partnerships and grow the program with them.

Contact Lex & White Studios
