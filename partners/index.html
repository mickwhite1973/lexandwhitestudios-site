WHY WORK WITH US

A growing software ecosystem.

Relevant audience

Reach users already interested in privacy, computing, software, online security and practical technology.

Useful placement

Partner offers can appear within the Lex & White website and selected customer-facing programs where they make sense.

Clear referrals

What partners receive

Discovery and referral.

  • Exposure within Lex & White Member Benefits
  • Tracked customer referrals where supported

What stays with you

Your customers remain your customers.

  • You control your products and pricing
  • You process your own customer payments
