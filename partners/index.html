Partnership options We do not expect every company to use the same model. We can start with whichever arrangement fits the partner's existing systems and commercial structure. Affiliate Partner Lex & White refers customers through an approved tracked link or campaign, and receives commission on qualifying purchases. Featured Partner A selected partner may receive additional placement within the Member Benefits area or agreed Lex & White communications. Promotional Partner Work together on a specific launch, member promotion, sponsored prize or campaign, with the arrangement agreed before publication. What partners receive Our goal is a simple relationship: the partner keeps control of its product and customer transaction, while Lex & White helps introduce relevant customers. Partner-side benefits Website exposure within the Member Benefits program. Tracked customer referrals where the partner supports them. Potential placement within selected Lex & White software. Future joint campaign opportunities as our customer base grows. What Lex & White does not need We do not need to hold the partner's inventory. We do not need to process the partner's customer payment. We do not need to take over the partner's warranty or product support. We do not need access to private customer browsing history.
